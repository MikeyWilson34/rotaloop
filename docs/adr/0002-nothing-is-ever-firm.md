# The schedule has no locked or "published" window

No Assignment is ever frozen. Skip Dates, Time Off, Declines and Rebalances may move any future Assignment, however soon it is. We considered a firm window (e.g. the next 4 weeks locked, with only Decline or Cover changing it). The product owner rejected it: volunteer schedules change constantly, and a lock would force awkward workarounds such as Skip Dates that don't shift turns. Instead, predictability comes from notification and confirmation. Any Assignment whose date changes goes back to needing confirmation, and the affected Members are told what moved and why.

## Consequences

- Recalculation must keep changes minimal: Time Off swaps only the absent Member and the next in line, and a Skip Date shifts each Rotation forward by exactly one Event.
- Every automatic change needs a schedule-history entry and a notification. These are what make the schedule feel predictable.
