# Feedback round 1 (2026-11-18)

Owner notes after using the app, plus decisions from Q&A.

1. **Collapsible "Needs your attention"** on Home. Header toggles collapse; collapsed shows the count; choice remembered per device.
2. **More repeat options** for Event Series. Already exist: weekly, biweekly, every N weeks, monthly Nth/last weekday. Add: every N days, monthly on a date (e.g. the 15th), every N months (applies to both monthly kinds), and an end (on a date or after N Events).
3. **One-off RSVP events** for groups that meet irregularly. Everyone in the Group is invited and answers Going / Can't (no Maybe, no +1s on day one). Live headcount. Optional Responsibilities on the same event (e.g. "bring food"). **Any Member** can create one; Admins can edit/remove any. Creator picks a reminder time (default 1 day before): it reminds Going members and nudges non-responders. Optional **RSVP deadline**, **location** and **notes**.
4. **Rename "Qualified Members" to "Team"**: "Sound team · 6 people", "Add to Sound team". "Team" is still avoided as a word for a Group.
5. **Duties stand out more than RSVPs**: an Assignment to a Responsibility is visually stronger (brighter/filled) than an RSVP-only invite.

Sequencing: 3 and 5 are prototyped in `design/prototype/` for owner sign-off before building. 1, 2, 4 build now.
